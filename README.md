NEXUS

A next-generation gaming community platform designed to unify social interaction, realtime communication, and gaming-focused communities into a single ecosystem.

NEXUS combines ideas inspired by modern community, streaming, and realtime communication platforms while focusing entirely on the gaming industry.

Overview

The project is being developed as a full-stack realtime web platform where users can:

Join gaming communities
Participate in realtime chat rooms
Share posts and gaming content
Interact with other players
Discover gaming discussions
Participate in future live streaming and multiplayer features

The platform is designed with scalability and realtime interaction in mind.
